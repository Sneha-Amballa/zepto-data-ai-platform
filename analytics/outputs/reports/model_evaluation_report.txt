Why Stratification is Required:
  The target variable 'survived' is imbalanced (approx. 38% survival rate vs 62% deceased rate).
  A standard random split might lead to a mismatch in the survival distribution between train and test sets.
  Stratification ensures that the training set and the test set contain the exact same proportion of survivors,
  preventing evaluation bias and ensuring model generalization.

Heteroscedasticity Assessment:
  Yes, heteroscedasticity is clearly present in the residuals plot.
  The residuals exhibit a classic 'funnel' pattern, where the variance of the residuals increases
  substantially for higher predicted ticket Fares. This occurs because cheaper fares are bounded near zero
  and show tight variance, while luxury class fares contain large residual fluctuations.
